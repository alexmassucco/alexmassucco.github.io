About

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Prof. Carola-Bibiane Schönlieb. I work on the mathematics of deep learning.

My research is in scientific machine learning, studying the theory behind neural models: their structure, generalisation, and optimisation landscapes. I aim to build frameworks that give provable guarantees on how these models behave, making AI systems more reliable and interpretable. More broadly, I am interested in the meeting point of pure mathematics and theoretical computer science.

Before Cambridge, I studied mathematics at the University of Pavia and IUSS Pavia as a student of Collegio Ghislieri. My PhD is funded by the Cambridge Trust and the Cantab Capital Institute for the Mathematics of Information.
